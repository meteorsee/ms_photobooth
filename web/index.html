Welcome to
Moment Studio.

Open the curtain and allow camera access when you're ready. Follow the pink pose guide, tap the large shutter, and the booth will count down through every photo automatically.

Your newest photo lands in the lower-left thumbnail while the live film rail fills up. Choose an open-source Instagram-style colour look or add Prism, Halation, Fisheye, and Grain as creative effects.

Everything happens in your browser. Your camera and photos are never sent to a server. Downloads are saved only when you choose.
