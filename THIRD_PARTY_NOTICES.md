# Third-party notices

## CSSgram

Moment Studio's Instagram-style colour looks adapt filter recipes from
[CSSgram](https://github.com/una/CSSgram), an open-source library released
under the MIT License. The adapted values are stored locally in this project
so the photo booth does not depend on a third-party CDN at runtime.

Prism, Halation, Fisheye, and Film Grain are Moment Studio creative effects
and are not CSSgram presets.
